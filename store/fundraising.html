Fundraising questions

Can I support multiple Scouts?

Yes. Place separate orders or assign support on each eligible product when campaign rules allow it.

How are Scout credits calculated?

Credit rules vary by campaign. Final percentages and eligible products are shared before a campaign opens.

Can I donate instead?

Direct donations are planned for a future phase. Contact the Fundraising Chair for current options.

How does pickup work?

Each campaign lists its pickup window and location. Final instructions are emailed after ordering.
