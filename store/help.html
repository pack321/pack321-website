Frequently asked questions

Answers for supporters

Can I change or cancel my order?

We will do our best before the campaign ordering deadline. Custom or already-submitted preorder items may not be changeable.

Where do I pick up my order?

Your campaign page and confirmation email provide the final location, date, and pickup window.

Can I make a donation without buying a product?

A dedicated donation portal is planned. For current giving options, contact Pack leadership.

Can I support a specific Scout?

Use the fundraising code shared directly by the Scout or family through Find a Scout.

Do Pack merchandise purchases earn Scout credit?

Only products linked to an eligible campaign receive campaign credit unless otherwise stated.

When will checkout be live?

This storefront is an architecture preview. Secure Stripe checkout will be added in a future phase.
